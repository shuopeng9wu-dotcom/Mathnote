				\begin{Def}
					Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an $\infty$-category. Suppose we are given objects $X,Y,Z\in \mathcal{C}$, with morphisms
					\begin{gather*}
						f:X\rightarrow Y\qquad g:Y\rightarrow Z\qquad h:X\rightarrow Z
					\end{gather*}
					
					Then, $h$ is called a \textbf{composition} if there exists a 2-simplex $\sigma$ of $\mathcal{C}$ such that
					\begin{gather*}
						d^2_0(\sigma)=f\qquad d^2_1(\sigma)=h\qquad d^2_2(\sigma)=g
					\end{gather*}
					
					In this case, the 2-simplex $\sigma$ witnesses as a composition of $f$ and $g$.
				\end{Def}
